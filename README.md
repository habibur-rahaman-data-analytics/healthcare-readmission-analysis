# Healthcare Readmission Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital encounter data to investigate 30-day patient readmission patterns and identify factors associated with readmission.

## Business Problem

Hospital readmissions can increase healthcare costs and place additional pressure on healthcare resources. This analysis explores readmission rates and patterns that may help healthcare stakeholders identify areas for further investigation.

## Business Questions

1. What percentage of encounters result in readmission within 30 days?
2. How do readmission rates vary across patient groups?
3. Are previous inpatient, emergency, and outpatient visits associated with readmission?
4. How do selected diagnoses and medication-related variables relate to readmission patterns?
5. What practical actions could be investigated to improve patient outcomes?
